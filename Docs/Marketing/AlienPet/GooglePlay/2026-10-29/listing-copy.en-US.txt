Alien Pet: Escape Puzzle

SHORT DESCRIPTION (70/80)

FULL DESCRIPTION (1289/4000)
Meet COghe, a curious little alien with a squishy liquid body and a big personality. Guide your new pet through 3D lab escape puzzles, then take a break together at Home.

A little pet. A big escape.
Tap to guide COghe, look around the glass room and work out the way forward. Climb bare glass, move crates, operate switches and ride lifts. Later puzzles ask you to split into smaller parts, work together and reunite before escaping.

65 campaign levels to explore
Discover new puzzle ideas across six chapters, with a boss puzzle at the end of each chapter. Rotate the camera to inspect the room, follow the connections between devices and try your own solution.

Care for your alien friend
Unlock Home as you progress. Feed COghe, share a little affection and discover its playful reactions. Try different snacks, play together and get to know the little creature behind every escape.

Make COghe your own
Unlock color inks and accessories along the way. Mix up to four inks, choose a hat and add tiny decorations that float inside your pet. Your saved look comes with COghe into the puzzles, Home and the main menu.

Small taps. Curious discoveries. One very squishy friend.
