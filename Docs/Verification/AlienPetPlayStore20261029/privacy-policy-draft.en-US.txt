Playing the game
The current Android build does not require an account, a name, an email address or a phone number to play. Progress, unlocked items, virtual Drops, customization and settings are stored locally on your device. The game does not provide an account-based cloud-save service. Gameplay events described below are separate from this local save.

Gameplay analytics
We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used and improve level difficulty and reliability. Events include starting, completing and failing levels, attempts and duration, tutorial progress, virtual rewards, item selections, and interactions with pet-care and advertising features. Event parameters may include level and attempt identifiers, item identifiers, virtual currency amounts, and the app or economy version. These events are not a recording of the screen or of everything you type or do on your device.

The Analytics SDK also processes installation/device identifiers and device/app information. [VERIFY AND DESCRIBE THE RELEASE CONFIGURATION: ADVERTISING ID COLLECTION, ADVERTISING FEATURES, GOOGLE SIGNALS, DATA SHARING, CONSENT AND ANY CHILD/AGE RESTRICTIONS.]

Ads
The game integrates Google AdMob to show ads, including optional ads that offer an in-game reward. Google's advertising SDK processes identifiers, approximate location derived from an IP address, ad/app interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Ads may be delivered and measured by Google and its advertising partners. Available choices and ad behavior depend on the consent and age configuration of the released version.

Configuration and service providers
Firebase Remote Config supplies game configuration values. It processes a Firebase installation identifier and technical context such as country, language, time zone and platform to return configuration to the app. This does not upload the complete local game save.

These Google services may process information outside your country. Their documentation describes their processing and safeguards:
